Who We Are

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a volunteer-driven non-profit organisation founded with a deep commitment to creating meaningful social impact across India. We bring together passionate individuals who believe in the power of collective action.

Through structured programs in education, environmental conservation, women empowerment, youth engagement, and animal welfare, we work at the grassroots level to address real challenges faced by underserved communities.

Our mission is to inspire individuals to contribute towards a compassionate, inclusive, and sustainable future — by working together for positive, lasting change.

Our Ongoing Projects

Community Service

Project SEVA

Supporting underprivileged families through food distribution drives, clothing donations, essential supply camps, and festive celebrations for those who need it most.

Providing dignity and basic needs to families in need
Education

Project BACHPANSHALA

Providing educational opportunities, digital literacy programs, and skill development workshops for children from underserved backgrounds — nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.

Bridging the education gap for hundreds of children
Women Empowerment

Project UDAAN

Empowering women through entrepreneurship workshops, vocational training, leadership development sessions, and financial awareness programs for long-term independence.

Helping women achieve financial freedom and confidence
Environment

Green Earth Drive

Organising tree plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns, and environmental awareness activities to build a sustainable and greener future for coming generations.

Planting seeds of sustainability in communities
Animal Welfare

Paws & Care

Feeding, rescuing, and providing basic medical support to stray animals. Raising community awareness around animal welfare and responsible co-existence with all living beings.

Giving voice to those who cannot speak for themselves
Youth Development

Young Leaders Program

Mentoring youth through personality development workshops, career guidance, volunteer training, and leadership activities that shape responsible citizens and future changemakers.

Grooming the next generation of compassionate leaders
Why It Matters

Social Impact & Purpose

Every initiative we run is designed to address a real, pressing need in our communities.

📚

Education & Skill Development

Access to quality education is a fundamental right. We bridge the learning gap for children and youth who face barriers to formal schooling.

  • Digital literacy & computer education
  • After-school tutoring and mentorship
  • Vocational and soft-skills training
  • Encouraging girls to stay in school
🌿

Environmental Sustainability

We work to protect our planet through active community participation in conservation, cleanliness, and eco-awareness.

  • Mass tree plantation drives
  • Cleanliness campaigns in public spaces
  • Plastic-free awareness programs
  • Teaching sustainable habits to youth
👩‍💼

Women Empowerment

We believe in a world where every woman has the tools, confidence, and opportunity to lead a self-sufficient life.

  • Financial literacy & entrepreneurship
  • Vocational skill training workshops
  • Leadership & confidence building
  • Safe spaces for women to share & grow
🤝

Community & Animal Welfare

From feeding families to caring for stray animals, we show up wherever compassion is needed most.

  • Food and clothing donation drives
  • Festival celebrations for underprivileged
  • Medical aid & feeding for stray animals
  • Youth volunteer training & engagement
